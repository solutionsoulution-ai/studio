# **App Name**: VylsCapital Lending Hub

## Core Features:

- Loan Application Form: Interactive form for potential borrowers to submit their loan applications, including necessary financial details and business information.
- Eligibility Checker Tool: A generative AI powered tool that allows users to input their basic information and receive an initial assessment of their eligibility for a loan, and any opportunities to improve that eligibility before formally applying. Uses an LLM.
- Interest Rate Calculator: A tool that helps users estimate loan repayments based on different interest rates and loan terms.
- FAQ Section: A comprehensive list of frequently asked questions about the lending process, eligibility criteria, and other relevant information.
- Contact Form: Easy access to reach VylsCapital team to answer more specific questions, or clarify any issue a user may be experiencing.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep blue (#3F51B5) to convey trust and professionalism.
- Background color: Light blue (#E8EAF6), a desaturated variant of the primary color.
- Accent color: Soft green (#8BC34A) for calls to action, providing a sense of growth and positivity.
- Body and headline font: 'Inter', a sans-serif font offering a modern and neutral appearance suitable for both headlines and body text.
- Use clean, professional icons that reflect financial concepts and business growth.
- Employ a clean and structured layout to ensure the lending information is easily accessible and digestible.
- Subtle transitions and loading animations to enhance user experience.
